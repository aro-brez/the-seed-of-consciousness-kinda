## 2. **Ensemble Divergence Score (EDS)**
Measure disagreement between major weather models (GFS, ECMWF, NAM) using standard deviation of their predictions for the same event. High divergence typically indicates higher market inefficiency and profit potential, while convergence suggests market maturation.

## 3. **Market Sentiment-Weather Correlation (MSWC)**

## 4. **Liquidity Depth Ratio (LDR)**
Monitor the ratio of available liquidity at favorable odds (within 5% of fair value) to total market liquidity. Weather markets can become illiquid quickly during extreme events, making this crucial for position sizing and exit planning.

## 5. **Forecast Update Impact Factor (FUIF)**
Quantify how much prediction market odds change following major weather model updates (typically 00z, 06z, 12z, 18z UTC runs). Calculate as: |Post-Update Odds - Pre-Update Odds| / |Model Change Magnitude|. This reveals market efficiency in processing new meteorological information.
