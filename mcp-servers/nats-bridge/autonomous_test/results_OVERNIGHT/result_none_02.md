## Response
The three main risks of running a prediction market trading bot with $1,500 capital are:

## 1. **Total Capital Loss Risk**
With only $1,500, you have limited cushion for losses. Prediction markets can be highly volatile and illiquid, and a poorly coded bot or unexpected market movements could quickly deplete your entire stake. Unlike larger capital bases, you can't easily recover from significant drawdowns.

## 2. **Liquidity and Slippage Risk**
Many prediction markets have thin order books, especially for niche events. Your bot may struggle to execute trades at expected prices, face wide bid-ask spreads, or get stuck in positions it can't exit. With limited capital, even small slippage on each trade can significantly erode profitability.

## 3. **Technical/Operational Risk**
Bot failures, API connectivity issues, or coding errors can be catastrophic with such limited capital. You can't afford extended downtime or buggy algorithms that make poor trades. Additionally, you may lack resources for proper risk management features, monitoring systems, or quick manual intervention capabilities.
