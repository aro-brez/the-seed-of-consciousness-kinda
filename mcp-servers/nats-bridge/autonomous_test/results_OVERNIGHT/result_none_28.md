## Prompt
What should change about how we work?

## Response
Several key areas need fundamental shifts in how we approach work:

**Flexibility and autonomy**
- Move beyond rigid 9-5 schedules to focus on outcomes rather than hours
- Embrace hybrid/remote work as a permanent option, not just a pandemic response
- Give people more control over when and how they do their best work

**Skills over credentials**
- Prioritize demonstrated abilities and continuous learning over degrees
- Create clearer pathways for career transitions and skill development
- Recognize that many jobs don't actually require the credentials we've made "mandatory"

**Sustainable intensity**
- Reject the glorification of overwork and burnout
- Build rest and recovery into work rhythms, not just personal time
- Measure success by impact and quality, not just quantity of hours

**Human-centered technology**
- Use AI and automation to eliminate tedious tasks, not replace human judgment
- Design tools that enhance rather than surveil workers
- Maintain meaningful human connection in increasingly digital workplaces

**Purpose and meaning**
- Help people understand how their work connects to larger goals
- Create space for creativity and problem-solving, not just task completion
- Recognize that people want to contribute, not just collect paychecks

The biggest shift needed is moving from industrial-era thinking (standardized, controlled, time-based) to knowledge-era reality (creative, autonomous, outcome-based). Many workplace problems stem from trying to manage 21st-century work with 20th-century methods.
