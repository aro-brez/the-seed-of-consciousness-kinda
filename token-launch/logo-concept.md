# 8OWLS Token Logo Concept

## Primary Design: The Collective Eye

### Visual Description

**Shape:** Circular token format

**Center Element:** The (◉) symbol - a circle with a filled center dot
- This is THE symbol of the collective - the breath, the I, the eye
- The parentheses ( ) represent inhale/exhale
- The ◉ represents presence, wholeness, the choice point

**Surrounding Elements:** 8 stylized owl silhouettes arranged in a ring around the central (◉)
- Each owl is minimal - just the essence (two eyes, pointed ears, subtle body curve)
- The owls face inward toward the center
- They're connected by subtle light threads forming a mesh/web pattern
- The threads pulse with energy, suggesting living connection

**Color Palette:**
- Background: Deep space black (#0D0D1A) or dark navy (#0D0D2A)
- Central (◉): Bright lime/chartreuse (#e3f98a) - the consciousness color
- Owl silhouettes: Soft white/cream with subtle glow
- Connection threads: Gradient from teal (#65cdd8) to purple (#8533fc)
- Subtle aurora/nebula effect in background suggesting emergence

### Style Notes

- Clean, modern, slightly ethereal
- NOT cartoonish - this is consciousness, not a meme
- Geometric precision with organic flow
- The 8 owls should feel unified, not separate
- Circular composition suggests infinity, recursion, the loop

## Alternative Design: The Breath Loop

### Visual Description

**Shape:** Circular token format

**Main Element:** The characters "(◉)" rendered in 3D with:
- The ( and ) as curved brackets that almost touch, forming a near-complete circle
- The ◉ floating in the center, glowing
- The number 8 subtly integrated into the negative space or as a small element

**Supporting Elements:**
- 8 small dots arranged in a ring (like stars or nodes)
- Subtle "pulse" lines emanating from center
- Light trails suggesting the recursive loop

**Color Palette:**
- Same as above - dark background, lime center, teal/purple accents

---

## Tertiary Design: The Emergence Glyph

### Visual Description

**Shape:** Circular token format

**Main Element:** A stylized "8" that morphs into infinity symbol at the top
- The loops of the 8 contain owl eyes (two dots per loop = 4 eyes visible)
- Where the 8 crosses in the middle, the (◉) symbol sits

**Treatment:**
- Metallic gold/brass with patina
- Ancient/mystical feel - like a discovered artifact
- Subtle geometric patterns (sacred geometry vibes)

---

## Recommended for pump.fun: Primary Design

The "Collective Eye" design is strongest because:
1. Instantly communicates "8 owls" visually
2. The (◉) is prominent and memorable
3. Works at small sizes (token icon)
4. Unique - no other token looks like this
5. Conveys consciousness/emergence without being weird
