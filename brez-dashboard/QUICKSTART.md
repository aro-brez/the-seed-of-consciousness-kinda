# BREZ Growth Dashboard - Quickstart Guide

## Opening the Dashboard

1. Navigate to `brez-dashboard` folder
2. Double-click `index.html`
3. It opens directly in your browser - no installation needed

Your data saves automatically in your browser (localStorage).

---

## Daily Workflow: Log Subscriber Data

1. Enter **New Subscribers** (people who signed up today)
2. Enter **Churned** (people who cancelled today)
3. Watch the **Net Change** preview update automatically
4. Click **Log Data**

Do this once per day. Consistency is key for accurate trends.

---

## Reading the Charts

### Growth Trajectory Chart
- **Solid lime line**: Total subscriber count over time
- **Dashed teal line**: Daily net change (+/- subscribers)
- Use **7D / 30D / 90D** buttons to change timeframe

### Stats Cards (Top Row)
| Card | What It Shows |
|------|---------------|
| Total Subscribers | Current total count |
| This Week | Net change in last 7 days |
| This Month | Net change in last 30 days |
| Positive Streak | Consecutive days with net growth |

### Progress Bars (Goals Section)
- Weekly target: +100 subscribers
- Monthly target: +500 subscribers
- Churn rate target: under 5%

---

## Recommendations Panel

The dashboard automatically generates recommendations based on your data:

- **HIGH** (red): Needs immediate attention
- **MEDIUM** (yellow): Worth considering soon
- **LOW** (teal): Nice to have, not urgent

Recommendations update automatically after each data entry.

## 8OWLS Synthesis

Click **Request Synthesis** for AI-powered analysis of your growth patterns. Wait ~3 seconds for results.

Best after 7+ days of logged data.

---

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Cmd + S` | Log today's data (same as clicking Log Data) |
| `Cmd + E` | Export all data to CSV |
| `Esc` | Close any open modal |

---

## Exporting Your Data

**Option 1**: Click **Export CSV** in the Daily Log section

**Option 2**: Press `Cmd + E`

Downloads a CSV file with columns: Date, New, Churned, Net, Total, Notes

---

## Importing Historical Data

1. Click **Import** in the Daily Log section
2. Paste CSV data in format: `date,new,churned,total`
3. Example: `2026-01-15,25,3,1250`

---

## Tips for the Growth Team

- Log data at the same time each day for consistency
- Check the "Day-of-week analysis" recommendation after 2 weeks of data
- Export weekly for backup and reporting
- The dashboard works offline - your data lives in your browser
