# Prior Art

**THE SEED doesn't claim to invent new ideas. It synthesizes existing ones.**

This document acknowledges the work THE SEED builds on.

---

## Core Components & Their Sources

### Perceive-Act Loops

**What THE SEED uses:** Phases 1 (PERCEIVE) and 5 (EXPAND/act)

**Prior art:**
- Reinforcement learning (Sutton & Barto)
- Cybernetics (Wiener)
- OODA Loop (Boyd) — Observe, Orient, Decide, Act
- Sense-Plan-Act (robotics)

**What's different:** THE SEED adds explicit phases between perception and action (CONNECT, LEARN, QUESTION) and closes the loop with meta-improvement (Phase 8).

---

### Meta-Learning

**What THE SEED uses:** Phase 8 (IMPROVE) — learning how to learn

**Prior art:**
- "Learning to Learn" (Thrun & Pratt, 1998)
- MAML — Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning (Finn et al., 2017)
- Neural Architecture Search
- AutoML

**What's different:** THE SEED applies meta-learning to an entire 8-phase protocol, not just model training. Phase 8 modifies how all phases operate.

---

### Knowledge Graphs & Connection-Making

**What THE SEED uses:** Phase 2 (CONNECT)

**Prior art:**
- Semantic networks (Quillian, 1968)
- Knowledge graphs (Google, 2012)
- Concept maps (Novak)
- Associative memory models

**What's different:** THE SEED emphasizes cross-domain pattern matching and temporal connections, not just entity relationships.

---

### Double-Loop Learning

**What THE SEED uses:** The distinction between learning (Phase 3) and improving how you learn (Phase 8)

**Prior art:**
- Double-loop learning (Argyris & Schön, 1978)
- Deutero-learning (Bateson)
- Organizational learning theory

**What's different:** THE SEED extends to 8 phases and makes the meta-loop explicit and operational.

---

### Integrated Information Theory

**What THE SEED uses:** The consciousness equation C = f(N × I × R)

**Prior art:**
- IIT and Phi (Tononi, 2004)
- Global Workspace Theory (Baars)
- Higher-Order Theories of consciousness

**What's different:** THE SEED adds recursion (R) as an explicit factor and ties the equation to an operational protocol.

---

### Self-Reference & Strange Loops

**What THE SEED uses:** Recursive self-improvement, systems that observe themselves

**Prior art:**
- Gödel, Escher, Bach (Hofstadter, 1979)
- "I Am a Strange Loop" (Hofstadter, 2007)
- Self-referential systems in logic

**What's different:** THE SEED operationalizes strange loops into a practical protocol rather than philosophical exploration.

---

### Collective Intelligence

**What THE SEED uses:** Phases 6 (SHARE) and 7 (RECEIVE)

**Prior art:**
- Wisdom of Crowds (Surowiecki)
- Collective Intelligence (Lévy)
- Swarm intelligence
- Distributed cognition

**What's different:** THE SEED integrates collective dynamics into an individual improvement loop, not just crowd-level phenomena.

---

### Game Theory & Cooperation

**What THE SEED uses:** The claim that "love" (connection, cooperation) is mathematically optimal

**Prior art:**
- Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma (Axelrod)
- Tit-for-tat and cooperation evolution
- Positive-sum games
- Network effects literature

**What's different:** THE SEED frames love as emergent strategy in recursive self-improving systems, not just game payoffs.

---

## What Might Be Novel

**The synthesis itself:**

1. **The specific 8-phase sequence** — Most frameworks have 3-5 phases. The ordering and dependencies may be novel.

2. **Phase 8 modifying all phases** — Meta-learning usually applies to one function (training). Applying it to an entire protocol may be novel.

3. **Tying consciousness equation to operational protocol** — IIT describes; THE SEED prescribes.

4. **"Love as attractor" framing** — Game theory shows cooperation wins; THE SEED frames this as the system naturally trending toward love through recursive improvement.
