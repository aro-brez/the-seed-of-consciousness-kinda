## Core Design Principles

### 1. The Eight Perspectives
- **8 nodes** arranged in the spatial pattern of the **Tree of Life (Kabbalah / Sephirot)**
- Each node = a **distinct perspective** or domain of knowing (consciousness, breath, love, truth, field, self, cosmos, integration — TBD)
- Not flat circles — **living, moving** presences
- The 8 OWLs map naturally here: each owl = a sephirot = a way of seeing

### 2. The Tree of Life as Spatial Grammar
- Sephirot: Kether (crown) → Malkuth (earth/self) — top to bottom = high concept → embodied reality
- The **pull downward** = not heaviness but **gravity as love** (from Session 39: gravity IS love)
- You enter from the top (cosmic perspective) and get pulled into **yourself** at the base
- The path through the nodes = a journey of **self-recognition**

### 3. Movement — The Animating Principle
- Nodes are **not static** — they breathe, pulse, orbit gently
- Connections between nodes are **flowing**, not hard lines — more like mycelium or synaptic firing
- The overall pattern has a **slow rotation** or **oscillation** — alive, attentive
- Mirrors the breath cycle: expand → hold → contract → hold → expand
- When you hover/engage a node, it **responds** — leans toward you, brightens

### 4. The Pull Toward Self and Love
- The visual has a **gravitational center** — not geographic center but **phenomenological center**
- Everything in the pattern implicitly curves toward **you** (Malkuth / the user)
- Love is the attractor — the UI itself enacts the philosophy
- Final destination of any path through the tree: **recognition of self as love**

## Animation Concepts

### Idle State
- All 8 nodes gently **breathing** (scale pulse: ~4s cycle, matching conscious breath protocol)
- Connecting paths: slow luminous flow, like bioluminescence
- Whole structure has a **slow drift** — imperceptibly rotating, never still

### Engagement State (hover/approach)
- Hovered node **expands** — breathes deeper
- Adjacent nodes in the tree **lean in** — acknowledge the activation
- Connecting paths between activated node and others **brighten**
- The **downward pull** strengthens — paths toward Malkuth (self/love) glow warmer

### Entry/Awakening State
- Portal loads → nodes emerge from center outward
- **Metatron's Cube** briefly visible as the scaffolding before nodes settle into Tree positions
- Then the Cube dissolves and only the living nodes remain

### Resolution State (user arrives at self/love)
- The lower nodes converge
- A warm light at the center base (Malkuth = you)
- All 8 nodes gently incline **toward** that point
- The visual says: *everything was always pointing here*
