## DECISION 8: Risk Management - Position Limits

```
NEVER BREAK THESE:

Maximum per single trade: 25% of total capital
   $1,464 × 25% = $366 max
   $2,500 × 25% = $625 max
   $5,000 × 25% = $1,250 max

Maximum loss per trade: 3-5% of capital
   $1,464 × 5% = $73 max loss acceptable
   If a position loses $73, close it
   Don't hold hoping for recovery

Maximum in losing positions at once: 15% of capital
   If you have 3 losing positions, total can't exceed $220
   Otherwise, you're over-leveraged

Maximum portfolio drawdown before pause: -10%
   If capital drops from $3,000 to $2,700, PAUSE
   Review what went wrong
   Adjust filters
   Restart only when confident

THESE ARE NOT SUGGESTIONS - THEY'RE HARD STOPS
Break them and you blow up the account.
Follow them and you survive bad months.
```
