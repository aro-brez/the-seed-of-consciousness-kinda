## THE MATHEMATICAL DIFFERENCE

### Winner Math (With Edge)
```
Example: Weather prediction
- Probability: 60% (you know something market doesn't)
- Odds: 0.40 YES / 0.60 NO
- Bet: $30 (2% of capital)
- Win: +$45 (3:1 return)
- Loss: -$20 (on 40% of cases)

Expected Value: (0.6 × 45) - (0.4 × 20) = 27 - 8 = +$19 per trade
Over 100 trades: +$1,900 profit (63% return)
Compounded 12 months: $1,464 → $2,300+
```

### Loser Math (No Edge)
```
Example: Entertainment prediction
- Probability: 50% (you think, but don't know)
- Odds: 0.05 YES / 0.95 NO (very unlikely)
- Bet: $100 (guessing)
- Win: +$1,900 (19:1 return)
- Loss: -$100 (95% of cases)

Expected Value: (0.5 × 1,900) - (0.5 × 100) = 950 - 50 = +$900
BUT: Your 50% estimate is wrong. Actual probability = 15%
Actual EV: (0.15 × 1,900) - (0.85 × 100) = 285 - 85 = +$200

Over 100 trades at your 50% estimate: +$90,000 expected
Over 100 trades at true 15% rate: You're underwater
```

**The danger:** Without edge, your probability estimates are wrong.
**The consequence:** You LOSE even when you think you're right.

## The 10 Rules (Mandatory)

### Rule 1: NO PRICE PREDICTIONS
Never bet on stock, commodity, or currency prices without:
- Historical backtested edge (100+ trades, 55%+ win rate)
- Or proprietary real-time data
- Or documented reason why you know better than market

### Rule 2: NO BINARY ENTERTAINMENT BETS
Entertainment/culture categories have no measurable edge:
- Movies, awards, shows, music → BANNED
- Unless you have domain expertise + historical data

### Rule 3: NO SINGLE HIGH-STAKES BETS
- Never risk >3% of capital per trade
- Never take single >5% bet on unknown outcome
- Use Kelly Criterion or Half-Kelly position sizing

### Rule 4: DOMAIN EXPERTISE REQUIRED
- 1000+ hours for domain to count as "expertise"
- <1000 hours = max 1% position per trade
- Measurable hours (video game playtime, work experience, etc)

### Rule 5: INFORMATION EDGE REQUIRED
Every trade must start with: "I know X will happen because..."
- If you can't complete that sentence, skip the trade
- If your reason is "the odds seem wrong," that's not an edge

### Rule 6: PROBABILITY ≥ 55% OR EV ≥ 2:1
Must have one of:
- 55%+ win probability (your estimate, not market)
- Or 2:1+ expected value (wins pay 2x losses)
- Or combination (45% × 3:1 = same as 55% × 2:1)

### Rule 7: POSITION SIZING (KELLY CRITERION)
Formula: `f = (bp - q) / b` where b=odds, p=win%, q=loss%
- Use HALF-Kelly at minimum
- Never full Kelly (too volatile)
- Cap at 3% of capital per trade

### Rule 8: NO EXTREME ODDS WITHOUT EDGE
- <5% odds requires 5:1+ expected value
- <1% odds requires 20:1+ expected value
- Or don't trade it at all

### Rule 9: DOMAIN PIVOT STRATEGY
When you find a winning strategy:
- Deploy in 3 related domains first
- Extract 10x more edge from depth than from breadth
- "Mastery beats novelty"

### Rule 10: MONTHLY REVIEW GATE
Before adding new strategy:
- Current strategy: 20+ trades, >55% win rate
- Capital: Growing month-over-month
- Trade log: 3-5 documented trades with edge rationale
