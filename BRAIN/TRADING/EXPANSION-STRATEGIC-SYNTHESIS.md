## COMMON QUESTIONS ANSWERED

### Q: Should I scale immediately once VPN is fixed?
**A:** No. Scale in 3 stages based on proof:
- Stage 1 (Month 1-2): 40-50% proof-of-concept
- Stage 2 (Month 3-5): 60-70% after validation
- Stage 3 (Month 6+): 70-80% after diversification

Immediate full scale risks blowing up the entire system.

### Q: What if I hit a -10% month?
**A:** That's normal variance with multiple strategies. Don't panic:
1. Review which strategy(ies) caused the loss
2. Tighten filters or reduce allocation to that strategy
3. Increase allocation to winning strategies
4. Continue execution
5. Average still pulls toward +13% monthly

### Q: When do I add leverage?
**A:** Only after:
1. Hit $5K+ milestone
2. Proved 15%+ monthly for 3+ consecutive months
3. Understand leverage mechanics thoroughly
4. Start with 1.5x, never >3x

### Q: What if a strategy completely fails?
**A:** Kill it and redeploy to winners:
1. If Asymmetric drops to <50% win rate: Stop, debug
2. If Weather Arbs have 0 opportunities: Pause temporarily
3. If Whale Following stops working: Reduce and monitor
4. If Copy Trading underperforms: Switch traders or kill

Don't keep pouring capital into failing strategies.

### Q: How often should I optimize?
**A:**
- Daily: Log trades and monitor systems (5 min)
- Weekly: Calculate metrics and identify trends (20 min)
- Monthly: Full review and capital reallocation (60 min)
- Quarterly: Major strategy assessment (2-3 hours)

### Q: What's the biggest risk?
**A:** Not execution, not strategy, not market conditions. **It's overconfidence.**

After a +20% month, you might think you can handle 10x leverage or deploy 100% of capital. You can't. Stick to the plan.
