## Risk Management (From CAPITAL-ALLOCATION-QUICK-REFERENCE.md)

**Hard stops (never break):**
- Maximum per single trade: 25% of capital
- Maximum loss per trade: 3-5% of capital
- Maximum in losing positions: 15% of capital
- Maximum portfolio drawdown: -10%

**Soft triggers (adjust when hit):**
- Win rate <50%? Tighten filters, reduce position size
- Capital dropped >10%? Pause, review, restart with adjustments
- Monthly return <10%? Analyze what changed
