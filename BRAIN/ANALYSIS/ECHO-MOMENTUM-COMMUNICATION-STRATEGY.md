## ANALYSIS: WHAT RESONATES vs. WHAT FAILS

### Current State (February 2026)

The dashboard currently shows:
- **Operators see:** "CAC is low, scale spending, watch these metrics daily"
- **Board sees:** "We added 327 subs? ...and that means?"
- **Investors see:** "Pretty numbers, but where's the proof of business model?"

### The Problem

**Three different audiences, one dashboard, three different interpretations.**

This doesn't mean we failed. It means we have an opportunity: **One dashboard can speak to all three IF we show them the same story from their perspective.**

## 1. WHAT IS THE ONE THING? (Clarity)

### The Core Story

Not: "Look at our CAC trending" (tactical)
Not: "Look at our revenue projection" (financial theater)
Not: "Look at our growth rate" (vanity metric)

**YES:** "This business model works. Here's the proof. Here's the risk. Here's the path."

### How to Communicate "The Model Works"

**In 5 seconds:**
```
"We acquire customers at $75.
They generate $340 in value.
Net profit: $265 per customer.
That math is sustainable and scalable."
```

**Current dashboard shows:** CAC + LTV separately. Requires executive to do the math.
**Should show:** Net unit economics prominently. "$265 profit per customer." Done.

**Why this resonates:**
- Investors understand unit economics instantly
- Board feels confident in scaling
- Operators know "this is worth doing"
- Finance can calculate runway with one number

**Action:** "Execute this playbook, measure this metric, report tomorrow"
- Current CAC band → spend recommendation

**Copy that resonates:**
- "Your CAC is in the SCALE band"

**Action:** "Approve/discuss this scaling scenario"

- Understand the break-even timeline

**Copy that resonates:**
- "We can fund this with our runway"
- "Break-even hits month 8, well within our 16-month window"
- "Recommendation: APPROVE aggressive scaling"

### For Investors (Quarterly/Funding)
**Action:** "Invest in this company because..."
- Proof of product-market fit (growth rate, retention)
- Proof of unit economics (LTV:CAC, payback period)
- Proof of scalability (deterministic model, not luck)

**Copy that resonates:**
- "We're 2.2x above industry LTV:CAC"
- "Payback period is 90 days (venture-scale efficient)"
- "At 75% win rate, we're capital efficient"

## 3. HOW SHOULD STATUS BE COMMUNICATED VISUALLY? (Design)

### Current Approach: Color Bands (Good Foundation)

```
CAC Band:      Color:           Action:
< $55          Green            SCALE AGGRESSIVE
$55-$75        Lime              SCALE MEASURED
$75-$90        Amber            MAINTAIN
> $90          Red              PAUSE & OPTIMIZE
```

**What works:** Instant decision support. No thinking required.
**What's missing:** Confidence level. "Are we SURE about this band?"

### Recommended Enhancements

#### 1. Status Badges with Emotional Resonance

```
NOT: "STATUS: OK"
YES: "✅ HEALTHY - Scaling profitably"

NOT: "Take rate: 14.2%"
YES: "⚠️ TAKE RATE: 1.8pp below target - needs attention"

NOT: "CAC trending $75"
YES: "📈 CAC STABLE - within optimal band"
```

**Why:** Emotional language builds trust and urgency without panic.

#### 2. The "Traffic Light" Hierarchy

```
LEVEL 1 (Most Important): "Can we afford this?"
├── Green: Yes. Break-even in month 8. Scale confidently.
├── Amber: Maybe. Break-even in month 12. Scale carefully.
└── Red: No. Break-even beyond our runway. Pause.

LEVEL 2 (Should Watch): "What needs optimization?"
├── Unit economics: On target
├── Take rate: 1.8pp below
└── Retention: On target

LEVEL 3 (Good to Know): Detailed metrics
└── CAC trend, channel performance, etc.
```

**Why:** First-time viewers get the essential truth. Deep divers get all the data.

#### 3. Progress Visualization: The Three Metrics That Matter

Instead of "February Goal: +327 subs, 75% complete"

**Show this story:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ARE WE WINNING?                         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ This Month vs Last Month:    ↑ 14%      │ ← Acceleration matters
│ This Year vs Last Year:      ↑ 281%     │ ← Scale matters
│ Profit Per Customer:         +$265      │ ← Sustainability matters
│ Days to Break-Even (scaled):  8 months  │ ← Feasibility matters
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Why:** These four metrics tell the complete story. Everything else is supporting detail.

#### Investors

- **Better:** "Cohorts are sticky. Month 6 retention is 78%. That's retention at scale." (proof)

#### Your Team
- **Current:** "February goal is +327 subs"
- **Better:** "We're on track for +281% YoY growth. Your work matters." (purpose)

1. **Confidence ("We know this works")**
   - Lead with unit economics, not projections
   - "This business model is proven"

   - "Break-even month 8 < 16-month runway = APPROVE scaling"

4. **Caution ("Here's what could go wrong")**

   - "Take rate 1.8pp below target. Watch, but not urgent."

## 5. WHAT TO SHARE vs. HIDE (Information Architecture)

### The Golden Rule
**Share:** Data that helps decision-making
**Hide:** Data that confuses without adding clarity
**Layered:** Detailed data available to deep divers, but doesn't clutter the main view

### Share Prominently

```
✅ Unit economics ($265 net profit per customer)
✅ Status relative to goal (are we on pace?)
✅ Trend (accelerating or decelerating?)
✅ Risk level (what needs attention?)
✅ Recommended action (what should I do?)
✅ Cash impact (can we afford this?)
```

### Hide by Default (But Available)

```
🔍 CAC by channel (show only if different from overall)
🔍 Cohort retention curves (show on demand, not default)
🔍 Hardcoded assumptions (in a collapsible details section)
🔍 Daily actuals (show week/month aggregate)
🔍 Intermediate calculations (show final results only)
```

### Why This Structure

- **Operators:** See the daily actionable signal (CAC band, today's action)
- **Board:** See the strategic signal (unit economics, can we afford it?)
- **Investors:** See the proof signal (LTV:CAC, payback period, scalability)
- **Analysts:** Can drill into detailed assumptions if they want

## 6. HOW TO FRAME WINS vs. LOSSES (Narrative)

### Current Approach
- ✅ Celebrates when ahead of pace
- ❌ Says nothing when behind

### Recommended Approach

#### When Winning (Above Goal)

**NOT:** "Celebration emoji, keep going"

**YES:**
```
📈 OUTPERFORMING
+327 subs vs +285 last month (↑ 15% acceleration)
On pace for +3,924 subs this year (↑ 281% YoY)
Recommendation: Capitalize. Test new channels. Document what's working.
```

#### When On Pace (Hitting Goal)

**NOT:** "Status: OK"

**YES:**
```
✅ ON TARGET
+325 of +327 goal (99.4% completion rate)
January performance: +285 subs
This month: Tracking 14% better. Maintain current spend strategy.
```

#### When Lagging (Below Goal)

**NOT:** Say nothing

**YES:**
```
⚠️ BELOW PACE (But not urgent)
+195 of +327 goal (60% with 12 days left)
Catch-up required: +11 subs/day for final 12 days
Action: Run last-minute campaign, test new acquisition channel, report daily
Risk level: MEDIUM (recoverable with action)
```

#### When In Crisis (Below By >30%)

**NOT:** Hide it

**YES:**
```
🚨 CRITICAL
+180 of +327 goal (55% with 8 days left)
Catch-up required: +18 subs/day for final 8 days
Root cause analysis: CAC spiked to $95, click volume down 22%, conversion flat
Immediate actions: Pause expensive channels, test discount offers, escalate for decision
Risk level: CRITICAL (may miss month, need board approval to fix)
```

### Why This Matters

**Loss framing principle:** Make it safe to be transparent about failure.

If the team sees "we can talk about misses," they'll surface problems earlier, when you can still fix them. If the dashboard says nothing about misses, people hide them until they're too late.

## 9. SPECIFIC COPY/LANGUAGE RECOMMENDATIONS

### Replace Technical with Human

| Technical | Human | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| "LTV:CAC ratio of 4.5x" | "Every customer is worth $265 net profit" | Concrete, emotionally clear |
| "92% monthly retention" | "Customers stay for 12+ months" | Time-based, relatable |
| "Payback period of 90 days" | "Investment breaks even in 3 months" | Decision framing |
| "CAC trending $75" | "Acquisition cost is stable and optimal" | Action-oriented |
| "Cohort analysis by month" | "Jan customers are worth $340; Dec customers $285" | Specific, provable |

### Status Language That Resonates

| Avoid | Use | Tone |
|-------|-----|------|
| "Status: OK" | "✅ On track and accelerating" | Positive, forward-looking |
| "Below threshold" | "⚠️ Needs attention (not urgent)" | Honest, actionable |
| "Risk detected" | "🚨 Critical: Requires decision" | Transparent, leadership-ready |
| "Continue current plan" | "Maintain strategy, watch for breakpoints" | Confident with guardrails |

### Recommendation Copy

| Weak | Strong | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| "Scale based on CAC" | "Scale aggressively. Unit economics are strong. Push to test limits." | Leadership confidence |
| "Monitor metrics" | "Watch CAC. If it rises above $80, we'll pause and pivot." | Specific guardrail |
| "Optimize take rate" | "Take rate is our biggest lever for Q1. A +2pp improvement = +$8M annual." | Makes priorities clear |
