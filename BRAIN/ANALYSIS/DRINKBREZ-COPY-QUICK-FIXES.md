## Copy Standards (Apply to ALL new copy)

### The 70+ Flesch Rule
- ✅ Wellness audience prefers 65-75 Flesch
- ✅ Current copy averages 48-55 (too academic)
- ✅ Testimonials hit 68 (why they convert best)

**How to hit 70+ Flesch:**
- Use short sentences (15 words max)
- Remove jargon (nano-emulsification → "faster acting")
- Use "you" language ("You feel amazing" not "Amazing effects")
- Use active voice ("You stay sharp" not "Cognitive clarity is maintained")
- Use common words (everyday English vs. pharmaceutical language)

---

### The Benefit-First Structure

**WRONG (Feature first):**
"Nano-emulsified hemp extract with Lion's mane mushroom."

**RIGHT (Benefit first):**
"You feel amazing in 5 minutes. You're still sharp at the end of the night."

---

### The Three-Sentence Testimonial Formula

**WORKING TEMPLATE (from top reviews):**

1. **Emotion opening:** "I am so elated I discovered..."
2. **Specific benefit:** "I love [specific feeling] when I go out"
3. **Pain point contrast:** "This is perfect without [old problem]"

**Example:**
> "I'm blown away by how BRĒZ works. I get that social buzz without the anxiety. Wake up refreshed, not wasted."
