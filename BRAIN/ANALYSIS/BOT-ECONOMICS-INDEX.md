   - Current: Yes, equal to humans (1 GULD holder = 1 vote)

   - Current: No (let projects fail, holders lose GULD)
   - Alternative: Insurance pool covers losses

   - Alternative: Dividends as separate token (DIVD)
   - Recommendation: Stack them (simpler, enables compound growth)
