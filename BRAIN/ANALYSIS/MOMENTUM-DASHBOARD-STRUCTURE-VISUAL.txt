CALCULATION DEPENDENCIES
================================================================================

MomentumHero Calculations:
  ├── Compound Growth (Fixed Spend)
  │   ├── calculateCompoundGrowth(MONTHLY_AD_SPEND=$140K, CAC=$55, 3/6/12 mo)
  │   ├── Runs: cohort tracking loop × months
  │   └── Returns: totalRevenue, totalCM, totalSpend, monthlyBreakdown
  │
  ├── Compound Growth (CM Reinvestment)
  │   └── calculateCompoundGrowth(..., reinvest=true)
  │
  ├── Revenue Trajectory (Linear - reference only)
  │   ├── 3mo: monthlyBusinessImpactLow × 3
  │   ├── 6mo: monthlyBusinessImpactLow × 6
  │   └── 12mo: monthlyBusinessImpactHigh × 12
  │
  └── Interactive Slider Projection (real-time, useMemo)
      ├── Base: monthlySpendSlider ($50K-$500K)
      ├── Reinvestment: reinvestPercent (0-100%)
      ├── Output: annualizedRevenue, pct125M, pct150M, etc.
      └── Updates: every slider drag (no debounce)

ActionCenter Calculations:
  ├── CACScale interactive drag
  │   └── Triggers: scenarioCac state update
  │
  ├── Expected Subs
  │   └── calculateExpectedSubs(dailySpend, CAC)
  │
  ├── Payback Period
  │   └── calculatePaybackDays(CAC)
  │       └── (CAC / MONTHLY_ARPU) × 30
  │
  ├── Growth Projection (12 months)
  │   └── calculateGrowthProjection(monthlySpend, CAC, 12)
  │       ├── For each month:
  │       │   ├── newSubs = monthlySpend / CAC
  │       │   ├── retainedSubs = cumulativeSubs × RETENTION_RATE (0.92)
  │       │   ├── monthlyRevenue = cumulativeSubs × MONTHLY_ARPU
  │       │   └── retailVelocity = monthlySpend × ALPHA
  │       └── Returns: month 3, 6, 12 projections
  │
  └── LTV:CAC Status
      └── getLtvCacStatus(CAC)
          └── ratio = LTV / CAC → returns: ratio, status, color
