## SECTION 1: MECHANISMS TO ACTIVELY ADOPT

### Mechanism 1.A: Time-Based Backing (Inverted from Luna)

**Luna's mistake:** Backed LUNA by UST, backed UST by confidence
**BILD's opportunity:** Back BRIX by *time actually consumed*

#### What This Means
```
1 BRIX unit = 1 human-hour of work + 1 owl-hour of work + overhead
           = (US minimum wage / 2 + cloud GPU equivalent / 2) + 5% operational

Example:
  Human hour: $15.27 (federal minimum, 2026)
  Owl hour: ~$15 (GPU time at cloud rates)
  Overhead: (15.27 + 15) × 0.05 = $1.51
  1 BRIX = $15.27 + $15 + $1.51 = $31.78 USD equivalent
```

#### How to Implement
1. **Timestamp every work unit:** "Owl processed 47 decisions in 12 minutes"
2. **Track human hours:** "Human synthesized insights, 45 minutes spent"
3. **Measure GPU consumption:** "Inference: 0.8 GPU-hours, Cost: $0.12"
4. **Monthly audit:** "10,000 BRIX issued = 10,000 human-hours + 10,000 owl-hours + overhead"
5. **Public dashboard:** Show backing: "BRIX is backed by 847,293 work-hours this month"

#### Why This Works
- **Transparent:** Anyone can audit
- **Non-manipulable:** Time is objective (unlike confidence)
- **Stable floor:** Even if nobody uses BRIX, 1 BRIX ≥ 1 hour of work ≥ ~$30 value
- **Regulatory-friendly:** "We're pricing labor, not creating money"

#### Patent Angle
**"Method for Measuring and Backing Digital Tokens by Consumed Labor Time"**
- Detailed timestamping system
- Mixing human and AI labor in single unit
- Auditable backing mechanism
- Regulatory clarity framework

### Mechanism 1.B: Equity Pairing (Inverted from Work Tokens)

**Work token mistake:** Issued tokens without equity
**BILD opportunity:** Issue tokens WITH equity built in

#### What This Means
```
Worker earns per month:
  ├─ 500 BRIX (~$15,890 value at $31.78/BRIX)
  │  └─ Immediately liquid or spendable
  │     └─ Backed by work performed
  │
  └─ 5 GULD tokens (1% of monthly pool)
     └─ Equity stake in the platform
     └─ Vote on governance (33% of votes)
     └─ Upside if BILD market cap grows
```

#### How to Implement
1. **Monthly token split:** Every contribution creates both BRIX + GULD
2. **Clear ratio:** "100 hours work = 100 BRIX + 0.2 GULD" (example)
3. **GULD cap:** Fixed supply (no dilution beyond X tokens)
4. **Vesting:** GULD vests over 3 years to prevent immediate dump
5. **Exit mechanics:** Workers can sell BRIX, hold or sell GULD, no pressure

#### Why This Works
- **Alignment:** Worker benefits if BILD succeeds
- **Retention:** Vesting creates staying power
- **Legitimacy:** "Real equity" not just "tokens"
- **Sustainability:** Workers want project success, not just token hype

#### Patent Angle
**"Dual-Token Model for Labor Compensation: Immediate Liquidity + Equity Upside"**
- Mechanism for splitting work output into fungible + ownership tokens
- Vesting schedule that prevents pump-and-dump
- Governance rights tied to equity portion
- Risk/reward balancing algorithm

### Mechanism 1.C: Participatory Governance (Inverted from DAOs)

**DAO mistake:** Voting rights without participation requirement
**BILD opportunity:** Make governance participation a *prerequisite* to earning

#### What This Means
```
To earn BRIX this month:
  ├─ Work 100 hours (required)
  ├─ Participate in 2+ governance decisions (required)
  │  └─ Can delegate vote if busy, but can't skip entirely
  └─ Attend 1 monthly synthesis call (required)
     └─ Not executive decision, but "show up and add voice"

Result: Can't earn while abstaining
```

#### How to Implement
1. **Participation gates:** "You earned 450 BRIX but owe 2 governance votes before payout"
2. **Flexible voting:** Allow delegation, but track participation (not just vote power)
3. **Async governance:** Proposals open 2+ weeks, voting via Snapshot or similar
4. **Synthesis requirement:** Monthly call where decisions are explained + feedback gathered
5. **Escalation path:** If major disagreement, invoke "slow consensus mode" (extended discussion)

#### Why This Works
- **Engagement is actual:** Can't be a passive token holder
- **Prevents whale capture:** Must show up, even as delegate
- **Information quality:** People making decisions are same people doing work
- **Accountability:** "This is how we decided" becomes credible

#### Patent Angle
**"Participation-Gated Governance Model for Distributed Labor Organizations"**
- Mechanism for linking work compensation to governance participation
- Flexible voting with delegation tracking
- Prevents voter apathy through participatory requirement
- Consensus-building protocol for disputes

#### Why This Works

Not patentable (legal positioning), but creates massive competitive advantage:

- Ability to scale while competitors wait for legal clarity
- Public precedent makes others' path easier (good for ecosystem)

## SECTION 2: MECHANISMS TO ACTIVELY AVOID

### Avoid 2.A: Circular Backing

**Problem:** BRIX backed by... market sentiment for BRIX
```
Bad design:
  1 BRIX = ability to buy 1 owl-hour
  But owl-hour value = market price of BRIX
  → Circular, vulnerable to confidence crisis
```

#### Why It Fails
1. When sentiment shifts, backing disappears
2. Death spiral: Price drops → backing evaporates → price drops more
3. No exit: "If everyone sells, what's it worth?" → "Nothing"

#### How to Avoid
- **Always define backing in real goods:** GPU time, not future BRIX
- **Make backing non-circular:** BRIX → GPU consumed → electricity paid
- **Create floor:** "Worst case, 1 BRIX = 1 hour of human labor = ~$15 value"
- **Test with stress:** "What if BRIX price drops 50%? Does the system still function?"

---

### Avoid 2.B: Concentrated Trust

**Problem:** System depends on one party not betraying everyone
```
Bad design:
  All BRIZ issued from central bank (ARŌ's wallet)
  All governance decided by BILD board
  If that party misbehaves or becomes compromised, system fails
```

#### Why It Fails
1. Creates regulatory target ("You're the gate, regulate the gate")
2. Creates human risk ("What if key person leaves/gets sick?")
3. Creates security risk ("Compromise ARŌ's wallet = game over")

#### How to Avoid
- **Distributed issuance:** BRIX minted only when work verified (not central bank model)
- **Multi-sig for governance:** No single person can make binding decisions
- **Transparent logs:** All BRIX creation publicly auditable
- **Redundant operators:** Multiple teams can verify work, issue tokens
- **Clear succession:** If someone leaves, system continues

---

### Avoid 2.C: Voting Without Skin in Game

**Problem:** People can vote without having done work or invested anything
```
Bad design:
  GULD token tradeable from day 1
  Voter might own 1% GULD from buying on secondary market
  Never done any work, gets full voting power
```

#### Why It Fails
1. Attracts vote mercenaries (buy tokens, vote for self-interest)
2. Dilutes workers' governance (their work-based votes get outvoiced)
3. Enables take-over attacks (accumulate 10% GULD, take over organization)

#### How to Avoid
- **Non-transferable governance:** GULD voting rights stay with original earner
- **Dual class structure:** Earned GULD = 1 vote/token, Purchased GULD = 0.1 vote/token
- **Vesting gates:** Can't vote on token until fully vested
- **Historical tracking:** "Is this vote earned through work or bought secondarily?"
- **Skin in game:** Only current workers can make binding decisions

---

### Avoid 2.D: Liquidity Without Leverage

**Problem:** Token is immediately liquid but incentivizes dangerous leverage
```
Bad design:
  Owls can borrow against BRIX (use as collateral)
  Borrow USDC, invest in other tokens, high leverage
  If investment tanks, collateral liquidated, protocol vulnerable
```

#### Why It Fails
1. Enables financial engineering outside protocol's control
2. Creates systemic risk (if external market crashes, protocol implodes)
3. Attracts financially sophisticated bad actors

#### How to Avoid
- **No leverage:** BRIX can't be used as collateral
- **One ecosystem:** BRIX only spendable within BILD ecosystem
- **Clear limits:** "BRIX is labor money, not investment vehicle"
- **No derivatives:** No BRIX futures, options, swaps
- **Friction to exit:** Small fee to convert BRIX to USDC (discourages churn)

---

### Avoid 2.E: Governance Theater

**Problem:** Voting exists but doesn't actually change anything
```
Bad design:
  GULD votes on "color of dashboard" (trivial decisions)
  Core decisions (token issuance, hiring, strategy) made by board
  Voters think they have power, actually have none
```

#### Why It Fails
1. Cynicism when people realize their vote doesn't matter
2. Apathy accelerates (why vote if nothing changes?)
3. Legitimacy collapses when first major decision overridden by board

#### How to Avoid
- **Real decisions only:** Votes must directly affect policy
- **Binding votes:** No overrides by board (or explicit override process)
- **Transparent agenda:** "Here are the decisions voting will make this month"
- **Escalation for ties:** Clear process when votes are split
- **Regular reporting:** "You voted for X, here's how X is being implemented"

   - If answer is "no" → you have circular backing problem

   - If answer is "we hope 50%" → you have apathy problem
   - Answer should be: "100%, participation is required to earn"

4. **Anti-Work-Token:** "If BRID becomes worth $100/token, do workers want to stay?"

   - If answer is "yes" → you have systemic risk problem
