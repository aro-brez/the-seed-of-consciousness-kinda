# ECHO's Quick Reference: Momentum Dashboard Communication
**For:** Product team, designers, marketers
**Use:** During implementation to keep communication principles in mind
**Created:** February 5, 2026

## THE CORE QUESTION

**What is the ONE thing this dashboard should communicate?**

> "We have a viable, scalable path to profitability with strong unit economics. Here's what we're doing today, here's what could go wrong, here's what we need you to know."

Every audience needs these three numbers. Everything else is detail.

```

    (Proves product-market fit)

2️⃣  PROFIT PER CUSTOMER

    (Proves business model)

    8 months (at proposed scaling)
```

These three numbers tell the complete story. Copy them to every section.

## COMMUNICATION LAYERS

### Layer 1: Glance (1 second)
```
✅ WE'RE WINNING
+281% YoY | +$265 profit/customer | Break-even month 8
```

### Layer 2: Read (10 seconds)
```
This month: On pace (+15% vs January)
This model: Strong (4.5x LTV:CAC, 2.2x above industry)
This quarter: One metric needs optimization (take rate +0.5-1pp)
```

### Layer 3: Understand (30 seconds)
```
Root cause | Action | Timeline | Risk level | Confidence
```

### Layer 4: Decide (10 seconds)
```
Recommendation: [APPROVE / MAINTAIN / OPTIMIZE]
Why: [brief rationale]
Contingency: [what if X]
```

### Layer 5: Deep Dive (if needed)
```
Details, assumptions, scenarios, competitive analysis
```

## THE TRAFFIC LIGHT SYSTEM

### Green (On Track, No Action)
```
✅ Unit economics working
✅ Customer demand proven
✅ Retention holding
✅ Runway sufficient
→ Copy: "On target. Continue current strategy."
```

### Yellow (Watch, Action In Progress)
```
⚠️  Take rate 1.8pp below target
⚠️  CAC trending up (still in band)
⚠️  Retention dropping (slowly, within tolerance)
→ Copy: "Needs attention. Action started. Not urgent."
```

### Red (Critical, Requires Decision)
```
🚨 Break-even beyond our runway
🚨 Customer churn accelerating
🚨 Market demand plummeting
→ Copy: "Critical. Requires board decision by [date]."
```

**Never show red unless action is truly required. Crying wolf destroys trust.**

## THE "SAY THIS, NOT THAT" GUIDE

| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Do Say | Why |
|------------|---------|-----|
| "LTV:CAC ratio is 4.5x" | "$265 profit per customer" | Concrete, memorable |
| "CAC is in the optimal band" | "$75 per customer (optimal range)" | Specific number |
| "92% monthly retention" | "Customers stay 12+ months" | Time-based, relatable |
| "Payback period of 90 days" | "Break-even in 3 months" | Decision framing |
| "Status: nominal" | "On track and stable" | Human language |
| "Risk detected" | "Needs attention (not urgent)" | Honest but not alarmist |

## STRUCTURE FOR DIFFERENT AUDIENCES

### For Operators (Daily)

**Lead with:** Action
```
✅ TODAY'S PLAYBOOK
Action: [specific task]
Timeline: [when]
Success metric: [how to know it worked]
If this happens: [decision trigger]
```

**Confidence:** Specific and high
("HIGH - validated across 150+ trades")

**Tone:** Action-oriented, specific
("Deploy $200. If CAC exceeds $80, pause.")

### For Board (Monthly)

**Lead with:** Decision
```
🎯 DECISION NEEDED
Proposal: [what]
Expected outcome: [upside]
Risk: [downside]
Recommendation: [APPROVE/DISCUSS/REJECT]
```

**Confidence:** Strategic
("HIGH - unit economics proven, market demand confirmed")

**Tone:** Professional, complete
("Break-even month 8, within runway. All contingencies planned.")

### For Investors (Quarterly)

**Lead with:** Proof
```
💡 PROOF OF BUSINESS MODEL
Product-market fit: [growth rate, retention]
Capital efficiency: [LTV:CAC, payback period]
Competitive moat: [vs peers]
Scaling plan: [timeline, capital needed]
Expected returns: [exit scenario]
```

**Confidence:** Evidence-based
("HIGH - 281% YoY growth, unit economics 2.2x better than peers")

**Tone:** Ambitious but realistic
("We're building a category leader. Here's the proof.")

## ERROR MESSAGES (What NOT to Say)

❌ "We don't know what will happen"
✅ "We have a contingency if X occurs"

❌ "Metrics are concerning"
✅ "Take rate is 1.8pp below target. We're testing optimization."

❌ "Risk is high"
✅ "If CAC rises 25%, break-even moves from month 8 to month 10. Plan B: reduce spend 30%."

❌ "We need more data"
✅ "We have strong evidence. Here's what we'd watch to confirm."

## EMOJI VOCABULARY

Use consistently across the dashboard:

```
✅ = Healthy, on target, good
⚠️  = Watch, attention needed, not urgent
🚨 = Critical, requires immediate decision
📈 = Growing, positive trend
📉 = Declining, watch
↑  = Going up (direction, not judgment)
↓  = Going down (direction, not judgment)
⏱️  = Time-bound, deadline involved
💰 = Money/financial impact
🎯 = Target, decision, approval needed
🔍 = Detail, clarification, drill-in available
```

**Golden rule:** Use 1-2 emojis max per section. Emoji overload = unprofessional.

## CONFIDENCE CALIBRATION

**HIGH confidence language:**
- "Validated across 150+ trades"
- "Unit economics proven at 2x scale"
- "Market demand confirmed (281% YoY)"
- "This is a known lever (precedent in [date])"

**MEDIUM confidence language:**
- "Test shows promise (need 2-week validation)"
- "Theory supported by data (small sample size)"
- "Historical precedent suggests this works"
- "Plan B ready if assumption breaks"

**LOW confidence language:**
- "This is an educated guess (needs validation)"
- "Theory untested at this scale"
- "Depends on [factor we can't control]"
- "We'll know more in [timeframe]"

**Never hide low confidence. Just be transparent about it.**

## QUICK WINS (Easy Improvements)

1. **Add the three magic numbers to the top** (+281%, +$265, 8 months)
2. **Convert jargon to human language** (LTV:CAC → profit per customer)
3. **Show historical comparison** (vs January, vs last year)
4. **Surface one risk explicitly** (take rate 1.8pp below target)
5. **Add a recommendation button** (APPROVE / DISCUSS / REJECT)
6. **Include confidence level** (why we believe this)
7. **Show yesterday-to-today delta** (is it getting better or worse?)
8. **Add "if X happens, then Y" triggers** (decision framework)

Each of these takes <1 hour but adds 10% clarity.

## FOR DESIGNERS

**Visual hierarchy:**
1. Hero metric (big, bold, emoji)
2. Supporting metrics (medium, white/lime text)
3. Details (small, gray text)
4. Hidden complexity (collapsible, gray, light)

**Color use:**
- Green (#6BCB77) = healthy
- Amber (#ffce33) = watch
- Red (#ff4444) = critical
- Lime (#e3f98a) = primary accent
- Gray (#676986) = secondary, details

**Typography:**
- H1: 24-28px, bold, color
- H2: 18-20px, semi-bold, white
- H3: 14-16px, regular, gray
- Body: 12-14px, light, gray
- Details: 10-12px, light, collapsed

**Layout:**
- Glance first (1 sec value)
- Then read (10 sec value)
- Then details (if interested)

## FOR EXECUTIVES (ARŌ)

**What to look for in the dashboard:**

1. **Do people understand it?** (Ask 3 people who haven't seen it before)
2. **Do people feel confident?** (Does it build trust or create doubt?)
3. **Do people know what to do?** (Are next steps clear?)
4. **Do people see the risks?** (Are problems surfaced or hidden?)
5. **Do you feel this tells your story?** (Does it match your vision?)

**If all five are YES, you're good. If any are NO, redesign that section.**

---

## THE ONE PRINCIPLE THAT MATTERS MOST

> "Make the decision easy. Bury the complexity. But make it available for those who want it."

Do this:
- **Easy part:** "Should we scale? YES, here's why."
- **Available part:** "Why? Click here for scenarios, assumptions, risks."
- **Hidden part:** "How did we calculate this? It's in the code."

Never force someone to understand the hidden part to make the easy decision.
