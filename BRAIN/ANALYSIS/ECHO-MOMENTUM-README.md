## The 5 Key Decisions

### 1. What is the ONE thing?
**Not:** "Look at our metrics"
**Yes:** "We have a viable, scalable, profitable business model"

### 2. What action should each audience take?
**Operators:** Execute this playbook with guardrails
**Board:** Approve/maintain/pivot this scaling scenario
**Investors:** Invest in this company because X

### 3. How should status be communicated?
**Traffic light:** ✅ Green (healthy) | 🟡 Yellow (watch) | 🚨 Red (critical)
**Status badges:** Emotionally resonant ("We're winning" vs "Status: OK")

### 4. What language resonates?
**Operators:** Action-oriented ("Deploy $200 on retention test")
**Board:** Decision-focused ("Break-even month 8, approve scaling?")
**Investors:** Proof-focused ("281% YoY, 2.2x better LTV:CAC than peers")

### 5. What's the story?
**Narrative arc:** Where we are → Why it works → What could happen → What could go wrong → What we recommend

## The Traffic Light System

### ✅ GREEN (Healthy, no action)
```
✅ Unit economics working
✅ Customer demand proven
✅ Retention holding
→ "On target. Continue strategy."
```

### 🟡 YELLOW (Watch, action in progress)
```
⚠️  Take rate 1.8pp below target
⚠️  CAC trending up (still optimal)
→ "Needs attention. Fix in progress. Not urgent."
```

### 🚨 RED (Critical, requires decision)
```
🚨 Break-even beyond runway
🚨 Churn accelerating
→ "Critical. Board decision required by EOW."
```

**Rule:** Never show red unless it's truly urgent. Crying wolf kills trust.

## FAQ

### Q: This seems like a lot of communication. Can't we just show the data?

**A:** Data without narrative is noise. Three people look at the same CAC number and see three different things. The dashboard's job is to answer the question they're asking, not just show numbers.

### Q: What if the real story is bad (missing goal, CAC rising)?

**A:** Tell the truth. Surface it early. Make it safe to talk about misses by handling them professionally. "Below pace but recoverable with X action" builds trust way better than hiding problems until they're critical.

### Q: Should we customize by user login?

**A:** Eventually. For v1, one dashboard should work for everyone because the story is the same. Just layer it (glance → read → understand → decide → deep). Everyone can go as deep as they want.

### Q: How often should this change?

**A:** Daily data refresh (LYRA keeps updating), but narrative changes monthly (when new data creates new story). The structure (traffic light, magic numbers) should stay stable.

### Q: What if we disagree on the recommendation?

**A:** Surface your assumptions and let people debate them. "We recommend APPROVE because we assume CAC stays at $75. If you think it'll rise to $95, let's discuss Plan B." Debate assumptions, not data.
