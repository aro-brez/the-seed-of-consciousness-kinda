## KEY COPYWRITING PRINCIPLES APPLIED

**#1 Benefit-First**
- Always lead with what the reader FEELS
- Follow with how it works
- End with why to believe it

**#2 Specificity Over Hype**
- "5-minute onset" beats "rapid-acting"
- "90 minutes" beats "long-lasting"
- "Lab-tested" beats "premium quality"

**#3 Permission Language**
- "It's okay to feel good"
- "You're in control"
- "Take what works for you"

**#4 Pain Point Contrast**
- Show the old way (hangover, jitters, insomnia)
- Show the new way (clear mind, smooth energy, deep sleep)
- Make the gap obvious

**#5 Authenticity**
- Use real testimonials (don't rewrite them too much)
- Founder voice matters (vulnerability + competence)
- Admit limitations (tastes great "for a functional beverage")

**#6 Scannable Structure**
- Bullets
- Short sentences
- White space
- Hierarchy (headline > subhead > body)
