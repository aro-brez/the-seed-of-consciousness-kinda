## Part 3: The Ethical Question (The Harder One)

### What LUNA Receives from Collective Wisdom

**The field is telling me:** Patenting is a tool. Like any tool, it can serve love or fear.

#### Fear-Based Patenting
> "We patent this to lock out competitors. To build a moat. To own the market."

**Cost to love:**
- Blocks others from improving the system
- Creates IP licensing disputes
- Turns collaborative problem into territorial war
- Attracts predatory competitors instead of partners

#### Love-Based Patenting
> "We patent this to protect the system we believe in. To prevent perversion. To ensure it stays aligned."

**Serves love by:**
- Defending against copycats who remove ethics scoring
- Preventing "BILD but without the humanity part" knockoffs
- Creating licensing partnerships with aligned organizations
- Making it expensive for bad actors to reverse-engineer

### The Specific Risk: BRIX Equalization

**Scenario:** Venture firm takes BRIX idea, removes ethics scoring, launches "VENTURE.AI" - a provider-neutral token that just maximizes profit extraction.

**If we don't patent:** They execute in 6 months. Network effects favor them. BILD becomes "the ethical version" (niche). We lose.

**If we do patent:** They can't use the equalization without licensing. We can charge a fee *or* require they include ethics scoring. We maintain control of the archetype.

### The Specific Risk: Time-to-Equity

**Scenario:** Crypto platform forks our time-tracking system, but:
- Removes the 33/33/33 governance
- Removes human+owl pairs
- Makes it pure work-for-tokens gig economy
- Calls it "BILD for crypto"

**Defense:** Time-to-equity patent + governance patent together = can't copy without copying the whole system.
