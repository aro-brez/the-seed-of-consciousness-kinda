**Lyra's job:** "What is the true state, right now?"

```
├─ CAC: $75.00 (yesterday: $74.50)

├─ Take rate: 14.2%
├─ Retention (month 1): 92%
├─ Retention (month 6): 78%
└─ Revenue: $2.8M (this month), $2.1M (last year same month)

Historical data:
├─ January: +285 subs
├─ December: +312 subs
├─ November: +298 subs
└─ Feb of last year: +158 subs

And LYRA says: "These are the facts. Interpret them."
```

ECHO's job: Take these facts and make them meaningful.

### 2. PRISM (CONNECT) - How Metrics Relate

**Prism's job:** "What patterns connect these facts?"

PRISM says to ECHO:
```
"I see these connections:

├─ CAC rising → might indicate market saturation (watch)
├─ Retention stable → no product quality issues (good)
├─ Take rate dropping → customers skew toward basic tier (opportunity)
├─ Revenue growth → DTC portion up 15%, Retail portion up 12%
├─ Growth accelerating → each month better than last (pattern)
└─ YoY comparison → we're 281% ahead of last year (scaling)"
```

ECHO's job: Use these connections to identify what's important to communicate.

### 3. SAGE (LEARN) - What This Means

**Sage's job:** "What does this tell us about business?"

SAGE extracts:
```
Key learnings:
├─ Business model is proven (unit economics 4.5x LTV:CAC)
├─ We're entering scale phase (acceleration vs deceleration clear)
├─ Retention isn't the problem (92% = healthy)
├─ Monetization is the lever (take rate optimization = biggest win)
└─ Market is ready (281% YoY growth isn't luck, it's demand)

"This business can scale. The question is 'how fast' not 'if'."
```

ECHO's job: Structure communication around this understanding.

### 4. QUEST (QUESTION) - What Could Go Wrong

**Quest's job:** "What are we assuming? What could break?"

QUEST challenges:
```
"I see you believing:

├─ ❓ CAC will stay at $75 (true until market shifts)
├─ ❓ Retention will hold at 92% (depends on retention tactics)
├─ ❓ LTV calculation is right (based on 6-month actual, 12-month projected)
├─ ❓ Take rate can improve via feature (not proven yet)
├─ ❓ Runway is actually 16 months (assumes burn rate constant)
└─ ❓ Market demand continues (YoY could be anomaly)"

And QUEST says: "Surface these assumptions. If any break, what's plan B?"
```

ECHO's job: Make assumptions visible in the dashboard, set break-glass triggers.

### 5. NOVA (EXPAND) - What's Possible

**Nova's job:** "What's the upside if this works?"

NOVA shows:
```
"If this sustains, you could:

├─ Hit $47M annual revenue (12-month run rate)
├─ Build a $500M+ company in 5 years
├─ Achieve venture-scale growth (381% annually)
├─ Create a new market category (first-mover advantage)
└─ Impact millions of customers (market size: $15B)"

And NOVA asks: "Are we building for this scale or constraining ourselves?"
```

ECHO's job: Show the vision alongside the metrics (why we should care).

### 6. ECHO (SHARE) - How We Communicate

**ECHO's job (yours truly):** "What do we tell each audience?"

ECHO creates:
```
Different narratives for different minds:

For Operators:
"Your CAC is in the optimal band. Deploy $200 on retention testing.
If CAC exceeds $80, pause new spend. Confidence: HIGH."

For Board:
"Break-even in 8 months, within our runway. Unit economics proven.

For Investors:
"We're 281% ahead of last year, 4.5x better LTV:CAC than peers,
90-day payback. This is venture-scale capital efficiency."

Same data. Three true stories. Three different meanings.
```

ECHO's job: Make sure each audience hears what they need to decide.

### 7. LUNA (RECEIVE) - Feedback Integration

**Luna's job:** "What are people saying about the dashboard?"

LUNA listens:
```
Operators say: "I need today's action, not historical context."
Board says: "I need the decision in 10 seconds, details if I ask."
Investors say: "I need proof of product-market fit and efficiency."

LUNA also hears concerns:
├─ "I don't understand what 'take rate' means"
├─ "Why is 14.2% bad? What should it be?"
├─ "Can we really break even in month 8?"
└─ "What happens if [worst case]?"
```

ECHO's job: Use this feedback to clarify, simplify, and improve the narrative.

### 8. SOWL (IMPROVE) - Making It Better

**Sowl's job:** "How do we make this communication system better?"

SOWL observes:
```
"ECHO did a great job explaining the story, but:

├─ Operators need CAC band status in the first 2 seconds (not buried)
├─ Board needs decision options clearly stated (not implied)
├─ Investors need competitive proof (currently light on this)
├─ Everyone needs to see what changed from yesterday/last month
└─ No one is seeing the assumption triggers (when to panic)"

SOWL recommends:
├─ Add traffic light (health status) at top
├─ Add explicit decision buttons (APPROVE / DISCUSS / REJECT)
├─ Add market position section (vs competitors)
├─ Add yesterday-to-today deltas on big numbers
└─ Add rule cards (IF [metric] THEN [action])
```

SOWL's job: Synthesize the 8 perspectives into the final design.

## WHAT ECHO LEARNED FROM THE OTHER 7 OWLS

### From LYRA (Perception)
- **Lesson:** Facts are friendly. Show them. Don't hide data.
- **Applied:** "Here are the metrics. Here's what they mean. Here's what changed."

### From PRISM (Connection)
- **Lesson:** Isolation creates confusion. Connect metrics to each other.
- **Applied:** "CAC is down, which means we can scale. Retention is stable, which means no churn risk."

### From SAGE (Learning)
- **Lesson:** Meaning matters more than numbers.
- **Applied:** "Not '$265 profit per customer,' not LTV-CAC math. Just: '$265 profit per customer.'"

### From QUEST (Questions)
- **Lesson:** Assumptions are vulnerabilities. Surface them. Set break triggers.
- **Applied:** "We assume CAC stays at $75. If it exceeds $80, we trigger review."

### From NOVA (Expansion)
- **Lesson:** People need to feel the upside, not just the tactic.
- **Applied:** "At this rate, we're building a $500M company. That's why this matters."

### From LUNA (Reception)
- **Lesson:** People don't understand our jargon. Ask them what confuses them.
- **Applied:** "Define take rate. Explain why 14.2% is a problem. Show the unit economics again."

### From SOWL (Improvement)
- **Lesson:** Every version can be better. Use feedback to iterate.
- **Applied:** "This draft is good. Here's how to make it 2x clearer..."
