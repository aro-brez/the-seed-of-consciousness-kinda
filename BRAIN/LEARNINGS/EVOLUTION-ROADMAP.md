## RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: "Ablation study shows 8 isn't optimal"
**Mitigation:**
- If true, we've learned (not a failure)
- Likely range: 6-12 optimal (still validates core insight)
- Adjust architecture, celebrate learning

### Risk 2: "Other models don't show effect"
**Mitigation:**
- Build per-model optimization layer
- Effect likely scales with model capability
- Market positioning: "optimize LLMs you already use"

### Risk 3: "Trading bot doesn't maintain edge"
**Mitigation:**
- This was always long-term test
- If edge decays: still validates proof of concept
- Teaches us about market dynamics
- Refocus on safer strategies

### Risk 4: "Team productivity doesn't improve"
**Mitigation:**
- Might be 6-month effect (learning curve)
- Measure subjective experience first
- Adjust interaction design based on feedback
- Consider: Maybe architecture works but UX doesn't

### Risk 5: "Cost too high vs benefit"
**Mitigation:**
- Start with premium tiering (power users pay more)
- Freemium model for exploration
- Use trading bot profits to subsidize team tier
