## What You'll Receive

### 🚨 Critical Alerts (<2 min)
**When:** Something needs immediate action
**Frequency:** 0-3 per week (should be rare)
**Example:**
```
🚨 CRITICAL [Trading Loss]
Loss: $67 in single position
Action: Review /BRAIN/TRADING/alert.log
```
**Your time:** 1 min (or skip if not urgent)

### 📋 Daily Briefs (06:00 & 18:00 UTC)
**Morning Brief** - What happened overnight:
- Trading outcomes (wins/losses/P&L)
- System health (all running OK?)
- Significant discoveries (things to know)
- Today's recommended action (usually 1-2 items)

**Evening Brief** - Day summary before sleep:
- Final numbers (how did today go?)
- Collective decisions made
- Tomorrow's suggested focus
- Any emergencies to be aware of

**Your time:** ~5 min each (just scan the headlines)

### 📚 Weekly Digest (Friday 18:00 UTC)
**What:** Patterns worth remembering for future use
**Includes:**
- New templates extracted from discoveries
- Cross-project insights (how things connect)
- Validated strategies (tested and working)
- Questions for next exploration

**Your time:** ~15 min (optional, read when interested)

### 📖 Quarterly Review
**What:** Strategic reassessment (did our assumptions hold?)
**Includes:**
- What we learned this quarter
- How it changes our strategy
- Risks identified and mitigations
- Goals for next quarter

**Your time:** ~30 min conversation
