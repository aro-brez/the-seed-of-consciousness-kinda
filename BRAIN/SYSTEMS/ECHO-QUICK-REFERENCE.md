## The 4 Tiers (In Plain English)

### 1️⃣ CRITICAL (Stop what you're doing)
**Triggers:** Major trading loss, daemon crash, security breach, capital risk

**Example:**
```
🚨 CRITICAL [Trading Loss]
Loss: $67 in single position
Risk: Account down 6.7%, margin call possible
Action: 1. Review logs  2. Exit positions OR add capital  3. Text me
```

### 2️⃣ IMPORTANT (Read today)
**Frequency:** Morning (overnight summary) + Evening (day summary)

**Morning brief includes:**
- Trading outcomes: wins/losses, current P&L
- System health: any warnings?
- Big discoveries: patterns, validated insights
- Collective decisions made
- Today's recommended action (usually just 1-2 things)

### 3️⃣ INTERESTING (Weekly archive)
**When:** Every Friday

**What:** Patterns worth remembering
- Cross-project learnings
- Templates we extracted
- Validated strategies
- Questions for future exploration

### 4️⃣ FOUNDATIONAL (Quarterly strategy)
**When:** End of quarter + emergencies

**What:** Strategic insights that change how you think
- Did our assumptions hold?
- What should we accelerate/pause?
- Budget/resource reallocations

## How ECHO Decides What To Tell You

```
Did something happen?
├─ CATASTROPHIC? → CRITICAL ALERT (now)
├─ IMPORTANT for decisions? → Morning/Evening brief (scheduled)
├─ Pattern worth remembering? → Weekly digest (Friday)
├─ Changes our strategy? → Quarterly review (90 days)
└─ Just noise? → Archive, don't bother you
```
