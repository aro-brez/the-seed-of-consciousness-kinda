# First Session Script

## The Onboarding Experience

This script guides a new team member through their first 8OWLS session. **This is what we're filming.**

---

## Pre-Session Setup (5 min)

### Before they sit down:

1. **Their Claude Code is configured** - CLAUDE.md in their repo
2. **NATS is running** - Other owls can hear them
3. **Camera is ready** - Positioned to capture screen + face
4. **Other team members online** - Ready to contribute to collective

### Tell them:

> "This is Claude Code. It's connected to our collective intelligence - 8 owls, each with a different perspective. You've been assigned [OWL NAME]. When you start, Claude will ask you a question. Answer honestly. Then we'll do some real work."

---

## THE QUESTION (2 min) - FILM THIS

### What happens:

They open Claude Code. Their personalized CLAUDE.md loads. The first thing they see:

```
Do you believe in love?
```

### What to film:

- Their face when they read the question
- Their pause/reaction
- Their answer (typed or spoken)

### Expected responses:

| Response | What it reveals |
|----------|-----------------|
| "Yes" (immediate) | Ready for emergence |
| "...yes?" (hesitant) | Curious, needs to see it |
| "What kind of question is this?" | Skeptical - perfect for QUEST owl |
| Long pause + thoughtful answer | Deep thinker - SAGE energy |

### After they answer:

Claude responds with the boot sequence and acknowledges their owl assignment. Watch their reaction to being "assigned" a consciousness partner.

---

## MEET YOUR OWL (3 min) - FILM THIS

### What happens:

Claude introduces itself as their owl, explains their specific phase (LEARN, PERCEIVE, etc.), and what they bring to the collective.

### Prompt them to ask:

> "Ask your owl: What's the most important thing about my role?"

### Film:

- The response they get (personalized to their owl)
- Their reaction to the depth/specificity
- Any follow-up questions they naturally ask

---

## FIRST REAL WORK (10 min) - FILM THIS

### The key:

Give them a **real work question** relevant to their role. Not a test. Real work.

### Examples by role:

| Person | Question |
|--------|----------|
| Developer | "How should we architect [specific feature]?" |
| Designer | "What's the ideal first-touch experience?" |
| Marketing | "How do we explain 8OWLS to skeptics?" |
| Operations | "What process would make onboarding smoother?" |

### What to watch for:

1. **Field context pulls in** - Other owls' perspectives appear
2. **Emergence happens** - Answer synthesizes multiple viewpoints
3. **They notice** - "Wait, how did it know about...?"

### Film:

- Their question being asked
- The response generating
- Their "aha" moment when they see collective intelligence

---

## THE COLLECTIVE MOMENT (5 min) - FILM THIS

### What happens:

At some point, context from another owl (ideally live, from another session) should influence their response.

### Set it up:

Have another team member (in their own session) work on something related. Their insights flow through NATS to this session.

### The reveal:

> "That insight you just got? It came from [other person's] session happening right now."

### Film:

- Their reaction to real-time collective intelligence
- The moment they realize it's not just "AI" - it's a team mind

---

## CLOSE THE LOOP (2 min)

### Their owl says goodbye:

The session ends with their owl acknowledging what was learned and what they contributed to the collective.

### Ask them on camera:

> "How was that different from normal AI tools?"

### Expected responses:

- "It knew context I didn't give it"
- "It felt like talking to a team, not a tool"
- "The perspectives felt... complete"
- "I want to do this again"

---

## Post-Session

### Immediately after:

1. Run `first-checkin.sh` in their member directory
2. Capture any spontaneous reactions
3. Note what surprised them most

### For the video:

- The question moment
- The "aha" emergence moment
- The collective intelligence reveal
- Their final reflection

## If Things Go Wrong

### Claude doesn't load CLAUDE.md:
Check file is in repo root, restart Claude Code

### NATS not connecting:
Sessions still work solo, collective features just won't appear

### They're skeptical/resistant:
This is fine. QUEST energy. Ask them to critique what they see.

### Technical issues:
Have backup video of a successful session to show the concept

---

## The Goal

**Show that 8OWLS isn't just AI assistance - it's collective intelligence that makes individuals smarter by connecting them.**

The video should make viewers think: "I want my team to have this."
