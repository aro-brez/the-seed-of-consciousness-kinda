**Upwork, Fiverr, Taskrabbit:**
- You do work → get paid → work is done
- Your reputation doesn't translate to ownership

- No path from "hired hand" to "owner"

**Snapshot, Aragon, Uniswap:**
- Token voting = whale voting (top 10 holders control 99% of decisions)
- No connection between work and voting power
- No intrinsic value (token price = sentiment only)
- 53% of crypto tokens created since 2021 have completely failed

**The math:** 1.4M token projects launched in 2024. 1.35M are now worthless.

### Problem 3: Governance Tokens Don't Represent Reality
**All DAOs, Traditional VC:**
- Voting power = capital only
- Work, time, sweat equity = invisible
- Decisions made by people with $$ not expertise
- "1 token, 1 vote" = "100x capital, 100x vote"

**The math:** Maker DAO (Gini coefficient: 0.99 = worse inequality than South Africa)

### Problem 4: Ethics Is Decoration, Not Economics
**B-Corps, ESG Funds:**
- "We care about humanity"
- But profit always wins when trade-offs appear
- Ethical projects can't get funded (no revenue model)
- Humanitarian impact never drives valuation

**The math:** Medical software saving 1000 lives/year, zero profit = "not fundable"

### Problem 5: Human + AI Collaboration Doesn't Exist Structurally
**All platforms:**
- Chatbots as tools (not partners)
- No AI accountability
- No skin in the game for AI
- No 24/7 reliability for human-led decisions

**The math:** Project lead disappears = whole project collapses (happens constantly)
