## 1. CROSS-PLATFORM ARBITRAGE (Risk-Free Money)

### What It Is:
The SAME event is priced differently on Polymarket vs Kalshi. You buy both sides for less than $1.00, guaranteed to get $1.00 back.

### Example:
```
Event: "Will Fed cut rates by 0.25% in March?"

Polymarket: YES = $0.48
Kalshi: NO = $0.49

You pay: $0.48 + $0.49 = $0.97
You get: $1.00 (guaranteed)
Profit: $0.03 per dollar (3.09% return)
```

### How to Execute:
1. Open Polymarket and Kalshi side by side
2. Find same event on both platforms
3. Calculate: YES(Platform A) + NO(Platform B)
4. If total < $0.97, that's guaranteed profit
5. Buy both simultaneously
6. Wait for resolution
7. Collect $1.00

### First Trade:
- Position: $500
- Expected profit: $15-35
- Risk: Nearly zero (only execution risk)
- Time to profit: Days to weeks (when event resolves)

## 2. HIGH-PROBABILITY BONDS (95%+ Certain Events)

### What It Is:
Buy outcomes that are almost guaranteed to happen, priced at $0.95-0.97.

### Example:
```
Event: "Fed meeting will happen on March 19, 2026"
(Not predicting what they'll do, just that meeting happens)

Market price: $0.96
Actual probability: 99.9%
Return: 4.2% in 3 weeks
```

### Best Markets:
- **Scheduled announcements** (Fed meetings, earnings dates)
- **Calendar events** (holidays, known deadlines)
- **Physical certainties** (sun will rise, seasons change)

### How to Execute:
1. Look for markets trading at $0.95-0.97
2. Research: Is this truly 95%+ likely?
3. Black swan check: What could make this fail?
4. If confident, buy YES
5. Position: $600-900
6. Wait for resolution

### Red Flags (Avoid These):
- Political outcomes (black swans common)
- Celebrity behavior (unpredictable)
- Markets that "seem" certain but aren't
- Anything trading at $0.99+ (not enough return for risk)

### First Trade:

- Expected profit: $30-50
- Risk: Low (but black swans exist)

### How It Works:
```
Market: "Will it snow in Austin, TX in March?"
Market price: NO = $0.98, YES = $0.02
Your analysis: 5% chance (meteorology data)
You buy: YES for $0.02
If hits: $1.00 payout (50x return)
```

### Strategy:
