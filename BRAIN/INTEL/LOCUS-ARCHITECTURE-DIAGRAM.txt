LAYER 4: CONSUMERS (Decision Makers)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONSUMER 1: CAPITAL ALLOCATOR
─────────────────────────────

    Subscribes to: locus.budget_allocation

    Flow:
    1. Receive allocation command
    2. Validate against risk limits
    3. Execute transfers
    4. Publish confirmation: allocator.confirmed_allocation

    Example:
    • Withdraw $50,000 from reserve
    • Send $18,500 to latency_arb
    • Send $14,200 to cross_platform_arb
    • Send $12,100 to high_prob_bonding
    • Send $5,200 to domain_expertise
    • Confirm execution


CONSUMER 2: DASHBOARD / VISUALIZATION
──────────────────────────────────────

    Subscribes to: locus.aggregated_readout

    Displays:
    • Real-time convergence gauge
    • Strategy alignment matrix
    • Capital allocation bar charts
    • Trend analysis (improving/degrading)
    • Risk alerts

    Updates every 5 seconds


CONSUMER 3: RISK MONITORING
────────────────────────────

    Subscribes to: locus.aggregated_readout, locus.convergence_metrics

    Monitors:
    • Convergence drops below 0.55 (DEFENSIVE)
    • Signal staleness (>30s)
    • Strategy disconnects
    • Anomaly spikes
    • Execution confidence drops

    Alerts on significant events

KEY DESIGN PRINCIPLES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. SIGNAL, NOT VOTE
   Strategies publish data (confidence, accuracy, direction, edge...)
   Not opinions ("we should allocate X")

2. CONVERGENCE-DRIVEN
   Allocation scales with agreement level
   Disagreement → lower capital automatically

3. WEIGHTED BY PERFORMANCE
   Historical accuracy weights current signals
   New strategies contribute less until proven

4. REAL-TIME
   Updates every 5 seconds
   Latest signals immediately reflected

5. TRANSPARENT
   Every decision explainable
   All inputs logged and auditable

6. RESILIENT
   Missing signals don't break aggregation
   Weighted average handles sparse data

7. TESTABLE
   Pure math, no black boxes
   Deterministic, reproducible
