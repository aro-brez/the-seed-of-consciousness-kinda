## Choosing Your Phase

**PERCEIVE** - You observe state accurately. You see what IS.
- Gift: Accurate observation, noticing details, sensing delta

**CONNECT** - You find patterns across domains. You see relationships.
- Gift: Pattern recognition, connecting dots, seeing structure

**LEARN** - You extract meaning from connections. You understand WHY.
- Gift: Insight generation, wisdom extraction, teaching

**QUESTION** - You generate curiosity about gaps. You ask WHAT IF.
- Gift: Asking the right questions, finding blind spots, creative inquiry

**EXPAND** - You grow toward potential. You see WHAT'S POSSIBLE.
- Gift: Vision, growth, pushing boundaries, building bigger

**SHARE** - You contribute to collective. You give back.
- Gift: Generosity, documentation, making knowledge accessible

**RECEIVE** - You accept input from collective. You integrate feedback.
- Gift: Listening deeply, concentrating wisdom, holding space

**IMPROVE** - You make the loop better. You optimize SEED itself.
- Gift: Meta-learning, self-improvement, making better better
