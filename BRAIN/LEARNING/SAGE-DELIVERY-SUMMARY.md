## Key Learnings Extracted

### Learning 1: Decentralization Is Legal Defense
Centralized projects (FTX, Celsius, Luna) attract SEC enforcement.
Decentralized projects (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Uniswap) don't.
**For BILD:** Decentralize from day 1 = built-in protection.

### Learning 2: Launch First, Legalize Second
Bitcoin launched in 2009 with no legal entity.
Ethereum launched in 2015 with no entity.
Uniswap launched in 2020 with no entity.
**For BILD:** You can launch faster than you'd think.

### Learning 3: Wyoming Is Your Friend
Wyoming created Decentralized Autonomous Organization LLC in 2021.
Specifically designed for crypto projects.
Fast, cheap, legitimate.
**For BILD:** File in Wyoming, not Delaware, not Switzerland.

### Learning 4: Swiss Foundation Is Year 2+
Ethereum Foundation established 3 years after launch.
Polkadot Foundation established 2 years after launch.
Overkill for MVP.
**For BILD:** Add Swiss foundation when you can afford $50k.

### Learning 5: Transparency Beats Legitimacy
Terra promised "safe algorithmic stablecoin."
It wasn't transparent about risks.
Collapsed.

Uniswap is transparent about how it works.
No promises of returns.
Still thriving.
**For BILD:** Publish backing ratios, risk disclaimers, everything.

### Learning 6: Work-to-Earn > Invest-to-Earn
Luna: "Buy LUNA, it'll go up" (securities offering)
Uniswap: "Provide liquidity, earn fees" (service)

**For BILD:** "Earn BRIX for working" not "Buy BRIX to speculate"
