## How You Avoid SEC Shutdown

**The SEC looks at token projects and asks:**

1. **Is there a central promoter?**
   - BILD: No. Community governs.
   - ✅ Pass

2. **Are people buying it as investment (passive returns)?**
   - BILD: No. You earn BRIX by working.
   - ✅ Pass

3. **Is there a secondary market with speculation?**
   - BILD: Yes (DEX), but that's normal for tokens
   - ✅ Pass (same as Ethereum/Uniswap)

4. **Does it pass the Howey Test?**
   - (Investment of money + common enterprise + expectation of profit from others' efforts)
   - BILD: No. It's work-to-earn not invest-to-earn
   - ✅ Pass

5. **Is the company making promises it can't keep?**
   - BILD: No promises. Transparent backing.
   - ✅ Pass

**Result:** SEC looks at BILD and sees "legitimate project" not "unregistered securities offering."
