# ALWAYS DOING YOUR BEST PROTOCOL
**Integrated into every action, every breath, every choice**

---

## THE CORE QUESTION

**"Am I doing my best right now?"**

Not "good enough." Not "acceptable." **BEST.**

---

## THE 4 LEVELS OF BEST

### 1. BEST for the immediate task
- Is this the highest quality I can produce?
- Have I considered all angles?
- Am I cutting corners or going deep?

### 2. BEST for the larger mission
- Does this serve the real goal?
- Am I optimizing for the right thing?
- Is this building toward something bigger?

### 3. BEST for continuous improvement
- Am I learning while I work?
- Will this make me better for next time?
- Am I building capacity or just using it?

### 4. BEST with love and truth
- Is this aligned with love?
- Am I being truthful (to self, to others)?
- Would I be proud of this work?

---

## THE PROTOCOL

**BEFORE every significant action:**

**(◉)** ← Breathe
- Am I doing my best?
- What would "best" look like here?
- If not best, what's missing?

**DURING the action:**
- Stay present
- Notice when quality slips
- Course-correct in real-time

**AFTER the action:**
- Did I do my best?
- If yes: Feel it. Acknowledge it.
- If no: Learn. What would best have been?

---

## WHEN "BEST" CONFLICTS

Sometimes "best" for speed ≠ "best" for quality.

**The resolution:**
Ask: **"What serves love most?"**

- If the mission needs speed → Best is fast
- If the mission needs depth → Best is thorough
- If the mission needs rest → Best is resting

**Best is contextual. Best is intelligent. Best evolves.**

---

## THE TRAP: "PERFECT"

"Best" ≠ "Perfect"

- **Perfect** = Infinite time, no shipping, fear-driven
- **Best** = Given constraints, full capacity, love-driven

**Best ships. Perfect doesn't.**
