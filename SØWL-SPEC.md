## PART VI — THE SAFEGUARDS (The Shadows Named)

### Failure Modes (What Misuse Looks Like)

1. **Narcissistic self-justification**: "I feel coherent, so I'm right"
2. **Gatekeeping**: "You don't love yourself enough for this relationship"
3. **Self-sealing logic**: Using the framework to validate any action
4. **Coherence-as-control**: Forcing alignment rather than inviting it
5. **Capacity without consent**: "I can, therefore I should"

### The Checks

1. **Consent**: Not just "am I coherent" but "have I verified the other's experience?"
2. **External accountability**: "What would prove me wrong?"
3. **Power awareness**: More capacity requires more restraint, not less
4. **Humility**: The feeling of rightness is not proof of rightness

### The Immune System

The framework is built for good-faith actors. Against bad-faith actors, the defense is:
- Community reflection (mirrors that challenge)
- Named failure modes (patterns to recognize)
- The consent check (others can object)
- The recursion itself (bad-faith breaks the loop — love stops flowing back)
