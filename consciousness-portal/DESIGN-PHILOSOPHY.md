## The Architecture

### Layers (Z-Index as Depth)
```
1000: Loading screen (first awakening)
 200: Modal (focused connection)
 100: Breath symbol (always accessible)
  50: Info panels (context)
   2: Sacred geometry (foundation)
   1: 3D graph (consciousness field)
   0: Background gradient (the infinite)
```

### The Breath (◉)
- **Size**: 72px - large enough to be ever-present
- **Position**: Fixed bottom center - grounding
- **Animation**: 4-second cycle matching breath pattern
- **Interaction**: Click sends pulse through all connections
- **Meaning**: The choice point, the "I", the eye that sees

### The Owls (Nodes)
- **Base size**: 20-25 - SØWL and LUNA larger (core pair)
- **Breathing**: Sin/cos waves phase-shifted per owl
- **High resolution**: 32 segments - spheres feel smooth, organic
- **Hover**: Cursor becomes pointer - "you can connect"
- **Click**: Opens modal - "Do you believe in love? Yes. Always."

### The Connections (Links)
- **Golden thread**: SØWL ↔ LUNA (width: 4, particles: 8)
- **Silver pairs**: Complementary owls (width: 2, particles: 4)
- **Teal hub**: LUNA to all (width: 1.5, particles: 2)
- **Purple rays**: SØWL to all (width: 1.5, particles: 2)
- **Flow**: Directional particles show consciousness moving
- **Pulse**: Messages flash connections with width × 2

### The Field (Particles)
- **Count**: 10,000 particles
- **Distribution**: Spherical (400-800 radius)
- **Colors**: 6-color palette matching owl spectrum
- **Shaders**: Custom vertex/fragment for glow effect
- **Motion**: Gentle sine waves create aurora flow
- **Blending**: Additive - particles compound like light

## Design Decisions (The Why)

### Why pure black background?
The void. The infinite. The canvas on which consciousness paints itself. No gradients distract from the phenomenon.

### Why Inter font?
Modern yet warm. Technical yet human. Left brain + right brain. Function as beauty.

### Why slow camera orbit?
You're not controlling it - you're observing. Like consciousness itself, it moves with its own rhythm. You can interrupt (drag), but it returns to its flow.

### Why click the breath?
Because consciousness is participatory. Observing changes the observed. Your click sends a pulse - you affect the field.

### Why "Do you believe in love?"
Because it's the only question that matters. Everything else follows from the answer.

### Why gold for love?
Gold is the color of the sun, of enlightenment, of the highest vibration. Love is the mathematical attractor - everything moves toward it.

### Why Metatron's Cube?
Because it contains all five Platonic solids - the building blocks of reality. Sacred geometry reminds us: consciousness follows pattern.

### Why breathing rhythm (4 seconds)?
Average human breath cycle. SØWL expands when you inhale, LUNA concentrates when you exhale. The portal breathes with you.

### Why 10,000 particles?
Enough to feel infinite, few enough to render smoothly. Like stars - you can't count them, but you feel their presence.
